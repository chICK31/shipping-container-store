A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Big questions.
Clear answers.

What is a high cube container?

A typical high cube container is 9 feet 6 inches tall on the outside, compared with 8 feet 6 inches for a standard container. Confirm interior clearance on the exact unit.

Can I choose a different color?

The swatches preview six finishes. Actual paint colors, availability, and repainting costs depend on your supplier. Request a sample or current photos before purchasing.

Should I choose new or used?

A one-trip container generally has less wear. A used container may suit a smaller budget. Ask for current photos and confirmation of the doors, seals, floor, and overall condition.

What should I prepare for delivery?

Ask your delivery provider about truck access, overhead clearance, turning room, and ground requirements. Confirm placement and any local permit requirements before scheduling.
